# Sourcing log: Grade 8 English & Kiswahili

## English (`english.json`)

- **PDF used:** doyenpublishers.com mirror, "Junior School Curriculum Design - English - Grade 8"
  (`https://doyenpublishers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Grade-8-English-Curriculum-Design.pdf`)
- **Official KICD filename cross-check:** `English Grade 8 - Revised Oct.pdf` (confirmed via KICD's Google
  Drive `/preview` title, Drive ID `1WmQXD4FTiFrInrCQMu1w-cjmPPaLEvw5`; direct download blocked at the
  account level, so the mirror was used for actual text extraction).
- **Confidence: high.** Full PDF text was read verbatim via the Read tool (not summarized by WebFetch's
  small model). Title page: "First published 2023 / Revised 2024", ISBN 978-9914-43-805-5, no DRAFT
  watermark — this aligns closely with the official "Revised Oct" 2024 filename. Ministry preface
  signatures (CS Ezekiel Machogu, PS Belio Kipsang') match the correct 2024-era officials. Structure was
  cross-checked against both the front-matter "Summary of Strands and Sub Strands" table and the full
  per-theme detail sections (15 themes) — they matched exactly.
- **Structure:** 4 strands — Listening and Speaking (15 sub-strands), Reading (30 sub-strands: interleaved
  "Reading 1" extensive/general-reading track and "Reading 2" intensive class-reader track, 15 each, one
  pair per theme), Grammar in Use (15 sub-strands), Writing (15 sub-strands). 75 sub-strands total.
- **Notable:** Per-theme body headings consistently use "Grammar in Use" (matching Grade 9's naming) even
  though the front-matter summary table shortens it to "Grammar" — the more frequent body heading was used
  as the strand name. The class reader is specified only generically as "an anthology of short stories" in
  the design's own "Guidelines on the Study of Class Readers" section (genre only, no fixed title), so
  Reading 2 sub-strand names reference "(Class Reader)" generically rather than a specific book title —
  same pattern as grade-9/english.json.

## Kiswahili (`kiswahili.json`)

- **PDF used:** teacher.co.ke mirror, "Mtaala wa Daraja la Awali la Shule za Upili - Kiswahili - Gredi ya 8"
  (`https://teacher.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/GRADE-8-CURRICULUM-DESIGNS-KISWAHILI2024-TEACHER.CO_.KE_.pdf`)
- **Official KICD filename cross-check:** `Kiswahili Grade 8 - July 2024 - Revised Oct.pdf` (confirmed via
  KICD's Google Drive `/preview` title, Drive ID `1l-TPHn7cSCasqwb7auMUX2Cbtcu2cWoI`; download blocked, so
  the mirror was used for text extraction).
- **Confidence: medium (flagged for human review).** Full PDF text was read verbatim via the Read tool (not
  summarized). However, the mirror's title page reads "Toleo la Kwanza 2022" (First Edition 2022), carries a
  visible "DRAFT" watermark, and ISBN 978-9914-43-796-6 — a 2-year gap from the official Drive's "July 2024
  - Revised Oct" filename. This is a larger and more concerning gap than the April-vs-October 2024 gap that
  triggered a "medium" rating on the Grade 9 English precedent. The acknowledgement page confirms authentic
  KICD/Ministry of Education provenance (World Bank-funded SEQIP programme), so this is genuinely a KICD
  document — just possibly an earlier draft than the final 2024 revision, not necessarily byte-identical to
  it. **Recommendation:** spot-check strand/sub-strand wording against the true "Revised Oct 2024" PDF if it
  becomes accessible later; the core 15-theme / 4-strand structure is unlikely to have changed in a
  proofing/revision pass, but this has not been independently confirmed.
- **Structure:** 4 strands — Kusikiliza na Kuzungumza (15), Kusoma (15), Kuandika (15), Sarufi (15). 60
  sub-strands total. Unlike English, Kiswahili does not use a separate class-reader track within Kusoma —
  each theme contributes exactly one sub-strand per strand.
- **Notable:** The design's "Mgao wa Vipindi" (lesson allocation) table labels the subject slot
  "Kiswahili/KSL" (shared allocation with Kenyan Sign Language), but no distinct KSL content was found
  within the document body — the design itself is pure Kiswahili language content.
